Location & Geography

A Land of
Remarkable Diversity

Situated at the very heart of Morocco, Béni Mellal-Khénifra straddles four distinct geographical zones: the majestic peaks of the High and Middle Atlas, the fertile Tadla plain irrigated by the Oum Er-Rbia River, the transitional piedmont foothills, and the phosphate-rich Khouribga plateau.

The region borders five other Moroccan regions — Rabat-Salé-Kénitra to the north, Fès-Meknès to the northeast, Drâa-Tafilalet to the southeast, and Marrakesh-Safi and Casablanca-Settat to the west. The A4 expressway connects it to Casablanca, while National Route 8 links Beni Mellal to both Marrakesh and Fez.

The region was formed in September 2015 as part of Morocco's territorial reform, uniting three former provinces with Khouribga and Khénifra into a single dynamic administrative region.

  • Fquih Ben Salah Province Agricultural heartland

Key Facts

Why Béni Mellal
Stands Apart

01 🏔️

Atlas Majesty

Over 65% of the region is mountainous, spanning the High and Middle Atlas ranges. Snow-capped peaks, cedar forests, and deep gorges define the landscape — home to Barbary macaques and rare flora.

02 💧

Water Wealth

15 major dams and 7 water transfer systems store 3.5 billion cubic meters. Hydroelectric plants generate over 1,860 million KWh per year — more than 70% of Morocco's national hydroelectric output.

03 🌾

Fertile Plains

The Tadla plain, irrigated by the Oum Er-Rbia River, is one of Morocco's most productive agricultural zones, growing cereals, sugar beet, olives, citrus, and pomegranates at scale.

04 ⛏️

Phosphate Empire

The Ouled Abdoun Basin near Khouribga holds 44% of Morocco's entire phosphate reserves, making the region a cornerstone of the national economy and global fertilizer supply chains.

05 🎭

Living Heritage

Home to the Zayane Berber tribe, ancient kasbahs, and authentic rural traditions, the region preserves Morocco's most genuine cultural identity — far from the tourist-commodified medinas.

06 ✈️

Rising Connectivity

Béni Mellal International Airport resumed international flights in 2024, with routes to Milan and Girona, opening the region to the world while the A4 expressway ensures swift land connections.

🌿 Agriculture

The fertile Tadla plain produces cereals, sugar beet, olives, citrus, and pomegranates. Milk and meat production are also substantial, supporting both local needs and national markets.

⚡ Hydroelectric Energy

Fifteen dams and extensive water infrastructure generate approximately 1,860 million KWh annually, positioning the region as Morocco's primary renewable energy producer.

🪨 Mining & Phosphates

Khouribga Province anchors Morocco's phosphate empire. The Ouled Abdoun Basin makes this region essential to global fertilizer supply, with lead-zinc and industrial minerals also present.

🌲 Ecotourism Potential

Spectacular Atlas scenery, cedar forests, traditional villages, and growing tourism infrastructure create significant opportunities for sustainable ecotourism development.

Tourism &
Natural Wonders

💦
Cascade d'Ouzoud

Morocco's most spectacular waterfall, plunging over 110m into emerald pools. Barbary macaques inhabit the surrounding olive groves, and rainbow mists delight visitors year-round.

🏰
Ain Asserdoun Springs & Kasbah

The historic Kasbah of Béni Mellal, founded by Moulay Ismaïl in 1688, overlooks the stunning natural springs of Ain Asserdoun — a uniquely hexagonal landmark mirroring Paris' shape.

🌉
Imi-n-Ifri Natural Bridge

A breathtaking natural rock arch carved by the Lakhdar River, one of Morocco's most dramatic geological formations, beloved by hikers and photographers.

🦁
Taghzirte Gorges

Twenty kilometers from Béni Mellal, an unforgettable 5-hour hike through oak-forested canyons where Barbary macaques roam wild, with rivers and wildlife at every turn.

🎿
M'Goun Massif

At over 4,000m, the M'Goun is Morocco's second highest peak. A haven for trekkers, climbers, and adventurers seeking the raw wilderness of the High Atlas.

🌡️ Climate Guide

Continental climate — hot summers, cool winters. Max 40°C in Béni Mellal; min 2°C in mountainous Azilal.

Spring

Mild & green. Best for hiking and waterfalls at peak flow.

Summer

Hot plains, cool mountains. Seek altitude for relief.

Autumn

Harvest season. Golden landscapes and ideal temperatures.

Winter

Snow on Atlas peaks. Cedar forests blanketed in white.
